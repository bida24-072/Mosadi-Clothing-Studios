> *"Where Botswana creativity meets contemporary fashion."*

---

## 🎨 About the Brand

Mosadi Clothing Studios is a tribute to the women who raised us. The name **Mosadi** means **woman** in Setswana — a celebration of identity, confidence, individuality, and the powerful influence of women in our society.

Rooted in Botswana heritage. Designed for the modern African.

**Founders:** Lethogonolo Brian Dintwe & Temogo Tale  
**Founded:** 2024  
**Based:** Gaborone, Botswana

---

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

An **editorial fashion-magazine aesthetic** using a warm earthy palette pulled directly from the brand's own designs.

| Colour | Hex | Where It's From |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| Deep Chocolate | `#3D2817` | Their studio backdrops |
| Warm Cream | `#EFE7D9` | Their beige trousers |
| Burnt Orange | `#D8552B` | Their headwrap artwork |
| Olive Green | `#4A5D3D` | Their green tees |

**Typography:**
- **Display:** Fraunces (variable serif) — editorial, fashionable
- **Body:** Inter — clean, readable
- **Accents:** JetBrains Mono — technical, modern
