The Story

Woven from our roots.

The Heritage Collection draws from Botswana's cultural fabric — patterns, colours, and symbols that have told our story for generations.

Every piece is a chapter. Every thread is a tribute. We merge modern fashion with elements of our culture and identity, creating pieces that are not only fashionable, but meaningful.

This is where MCS seeks to merge modern fashion with elements of our culture and identity — celebrating Botswana talent, creativity and originality on both a local and international platform.
