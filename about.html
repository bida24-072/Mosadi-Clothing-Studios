Our Story

A tribute to our mothers, our sisters, and every woman who shaped us.

The Beginning

A tribute to our mothers.

Mosadi Clothing Studios (MCS) is a proudly Botswana-born fashion brand founded by Lethogonolo Brian Dintwe and Temogo Tale, driven by a shared passion for creativity, fashion, culture, and contemporary African style.

The name Mosadi, meaning woman, represents the inspiration behind our brand — celebrating identity, confidence, individuality, and the powerful influence of women in our society.

Both founders were raised by single mothers. Mothers who fought to protect them and take them to school. Mothers who gave them a better future. The duo created this line to celebrate them — and other women in society.

Mosadi celebrates the Motswana woman not only for how she looks, but for who she is. What she carries. What she has overcome. What she represents to the next generation.
