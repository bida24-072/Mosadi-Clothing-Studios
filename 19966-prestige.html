The Collection

Born from heritage.

1966 Prestige is a collection that pays homage to where we come from while celebrating where we are going. Every piece carries a piece of our history, reimagined for the modern Motswana man.

Featuring the iconic Mosadi headwrap artwork, the Botswana map, and hand-drawn patterns — 1966 Prestige captures a moment when heritage and modernity stand shoulder to shoulder.

It's not nostalgia. It's a statement — that who we were has always been who we are.
