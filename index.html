Our Story

A tribute to the women who raised us.

Mosadi Clothing Studios was founded by Lethogonolo Brian Dintwe and Temogo Tale — two friends raised by single mothers who fought to protect them, educate them, and give them a better future.

The name Mosadi means woman in Setswana. It is a tribute to our mothers and sisters. To every Motswana woman — not for how she looks, but for who she is. What she carries. What she has overcome. What she represents to the next generation.

And while Mosadi begins with women, it embraces everyone — creating designs that speak to both women and men, to families, and to a new generation of African storytellers.

— Lethogonolo & Temogo

Our Values & Core

What we stand for,
in every thread.

01

Creativity & Innovation

We embrace originality and continuously explore new ideas, designs, and fashion concepts that keep MCS contemporary and relevant.

02

Botswana Identity

We draw inspiration from our heritage, people, colours, and culture — using fashion as a platform to celebrate and showcase Botswana.

03

Quality & Craftsmanship

Committed to producing apparel that reflects attention to detail, quality materials, strong finishing, and lasting value.

04

Empowerment

Inspired by the strength and influence of women. We believe fashion can empower individuals to express their identity, confidence, and individuality.

05

Inclusivity

Our vision is to create fashion for diverse individuals — with collections catering to both women and men, and different lifestyles.

Authenticity

We believe in creating a distinctive MCS identity rather than simply following trends. Every collection should have a story, purpose, and personality.

07

Growth & Excellence

We continuously strive to improve our designs, business practices, and customer experience — building MCS into a competitive Botswana fashion brand.
