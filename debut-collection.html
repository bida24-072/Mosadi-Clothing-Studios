The Story

Where it all began.

The Debut Collection is where Mosadi Clothing Studios started — a celebration of women, craft, and the unmistakable Mosadi silhouette that began it all.

Founded by Lethogonolo Brian Dintwe and Temogo Tale — two friends raised by single mothers — the Debut Collection was created as a tribute to those mothers, and to women everywhere.

Every piece from this collection carries the DNA of who we are. Mosadi celebrates the Motswana woman not only for how she looks, but for who she is, what she carries, what she has overcome and what she represents to the next generation.
